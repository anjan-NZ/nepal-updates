Source status

Is every source working?

Every official site is checked every 8 hours. Working means its notice list loaded on the last check. Check means it came back empty once. Broken means it came back empty twice in a row, which usually means the site moved or changed its layout. A source with no new notices for weeks is usually just quiet.
